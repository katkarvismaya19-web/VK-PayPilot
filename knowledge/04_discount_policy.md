# Store Discount Policy

## Hard limits
No automated offer may exceed 20 percent off. Offers above 15 percent require merchant approval before sending.
Discounts must never push the order below a 10 percent gross margin. For a product with a 30 percent margin, the maximum safe discount is therefore 20 percent; for a 25 percent margin product it is 15 percent.

## Who gets what
Champions and Loyal customers, your best customers, do not receive percentage discounts from the agent; use non-monetary rewards such as early access or loyalty points instead.
At Risk and Can't Lose Them customers may receive up to 15 percent.
Hibernating customers may receive up to 10 percent.
Abandoned carts may receive up to 10 percent, and only in the second reminder.
Failed payments receive no discount.

## Offer mechanics
Every agent-generated offer uses a unique single-use code tied to the customer, valid for 7 days. This prevents code leakage to coupon sites and allows exact attribution of revenue to the action that created it.
