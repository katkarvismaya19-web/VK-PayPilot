# Customer Messaging and Compliance Rules

## Consent
Only contact customers who have opted in to marketing messages. Transactional messages, such as a payment retry link for a payment the customer initiated, may be sent without marketing consent.

## Frequency caps
No customer should receive more than 2 agent-initiated messages per week (any rolling 7-day period) across all channels, so message frequency never feels like spam. If a customer is eligible for several actions, send only the highest-value one.

## Quiet hours
Do not send promotional messages at night: nothing between 9 PM and 9 AM in the customer's local time. Messages created during quiet hours are scheduled for 9 AM. Transactional messages such as payment retry links may be sent at any time. In India, commercial communication rules restrict promotional calls and messages to customers who have not consented, so keep promotional messaging to opted-in customers and business hours.

## Channel choice
Email is the default channel. WhatsApp is reserved for high-value carts, payment retries and customers who have previously engaged on WhatsApp. SMS is used only for payment links when no email is available.

## Tone
Messages should be short, personal and specific: use the customer's first name, name the actual products, and include one clear action. Never use false urgency such as fake countdown timers or invented stock scarcity.
