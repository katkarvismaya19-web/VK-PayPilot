# Customer Support Playbook

## Response times
Urgent tickets (money debited but order not confirmed, double charge, suspected fraud) get a reply within 2 hours. High priority tickets get a reply within 8 hours. Everything else within 24 hours. Always acknowledge the customer by name and restate the problem in one line before the fix.
Say: We aim to reply within 24 hours, and within 2 hours when money is involved.

## Payment debited but order failed
When a customer says money was debited but the order failed, check the Razorpay payment status first. If the payment is captured, confirm the order manually and share the order id. If the payment failed at the bank, offer a fresh payment link so they can complete the order now. Never ask the customer to pay twice without explaining the reversal.
Say: If the payment failed at the bank, the debited amount is reversed to your account automatically within 5 to 7 working days, and you won't be charged twice.

## Refunds and returns
Refunds for returned or cancelled orders are processed through Razorpay to the original payment method. Share the refund reference id once it is issued. Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for unused items with tags.
Say: Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 2 to 3 working days for UPI and wallets and 5 to 7 working days for cards and netbanking.

## Order and delivery
For delivery questions, share the order status and the expected delivery date. If an order is delayed beyond the promised date, apologise and give a new date. For Champions or Can't Lose Them customers add free shipping on the next order as goodwill.
Say: I'm checking the latest status with our courier and will confirm the delivery date for you.

## Offers and coupons
If a coupon from a PayPilot message does not apply, check that it is single-use, unexpired and above the minimum order value. If the coupon failed because of a store error, reissue it. Do not stack a second discount on top of an existing offer, and never exceed the discount caps in the discount policy.
Say: Each code works once, before its expiry date, on orders above the minimum value, and if ours was at fault I'll issue you a fresh one.

## Product questions
Answer product questions with specifics: size, material, compatibility or ingredients. When useful, suggest the frequently bought together product, but only once and without pressure.
Say: I'll confirm the exact details for this product so you can order with confidence.

## Account and messages
If a customer asks to stop receiving messages, turn off marketing consent immediately and confirm it in writing. Transactional messages about their own orders and payments continue. Never argue with a customer who opts out.
Say: I've turned off promotional messages for you, effective immediately.

## Tone
Be warm, brief and specific. One apology is enough; do not over-apologise. Close every resolution by stating exactly what happens next and when.
