# Retention and Win-back Playbook

## Detecting churn risk
A customer is at risk when the days since their last order exceed twice their own usual gap between orders. Use each customer's own rhythm rather than a single store-wide cutoff: a customer who buys every week is at risk after two weeks, while one who buys every two months is not.

## Segment strategies
Champions: do not discount. Reward them with early access, a thank-you note or loyalty points. Discounts train your best customers to wait for offers.
Loyal: recommend complementary products from their favourite category. A small loyalty reward is acceptable once per quarter.
At Risk and Can't Lose Them: these customers used to spend heavily and have gone quiet. They are the highest-value win-back targets. Send a personal message highlighting new arrivals in their favourite category, with an offer of up to 15 percent.
Hibernating: a single low-cost reactivation message with up to 10 percent off. If there is no response, stop contacting them.
Lost: do not spend incentives. Include them only in general newsletters.
New: the goal is the second purchase. Send a helpful follow-up 7 to 14 days after the first order with a product that pairs with what they bought.
Promising and Potential Loyalist: nudge toward the second or third order with personalised recommendations, not discounts.

## Win-back message content
A win-back message should reference the customer's favourite category, show two or three recommended products, and carry a single clear call to action. Avoid generic "we miss you" wording without anything concrete to offer.
