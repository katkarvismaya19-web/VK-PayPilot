# Upsell and Cross-sell Playbook

## Product pairing
Use market-basket analysis to find products frequently bought together. A pair with lift above 1.5 is a meaningful association and can be recommended as "customers also bought". Pairs with lift below 1.2 are close to chance and should not be promoted.

## Next best product
For an active customer, recommend popular products in their top one or two categories that they have not bought yet. Do not recommend products they already own unless the product is consumable (Grocery and Beauty categories are consumable and can be recommended for repurchase).

## Replenishment reminders
Consumable products should get a replenishment reminder near the customer's usual reorder interval. These convert well because they meet a real need and require no discount.

## Average order value
To raise average order value, suggest a complementary product that brings the cart just above the free-shipping threshold of 999 rupees, rather than offering a discount.
